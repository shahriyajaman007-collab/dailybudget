Everything a household budget needs

  • A daily spending envelope

    Set a daily limit, see what is left, and get warnings at 50%, 80% and 100%. Unused money can roll over within the month.

  • Income and expenses

    Record transactions by hand with categories, payment method and notes. Search, filter and sort by any date range.

  • Monthly and category budgets

    Cap groceries, restaurants or the whole month and watch budget against actual spending as it happens.

  • Bills that never double count

    Track rent, utilities and subscriptions. Paying a bill records one linked expense and moves it to the next due date.

  • Savings goals

    Plan for an emergency fund, a car or a trip, and see how much of your balance is set aside versus free to use.

  • Reports and CSV export

    Charts for income against expenses, daily and weekly spending, categories and budgets. Download your data at any time.

Privacy and security

How your records are protected

  • Each account can read and change only its own records. The database enforces this with row level security, not the screen.
  • Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by this app.
  • Nothing you enter is sent to advertisers or AI services.
  • You can export everything or delete your account and all of its data from Settings.

Good to know

  • DailyBudget does not connect to banks. Balances and savings are figures you enter, not amounts verified by a financial institution.
  • Never enter bank passwords, full card numbers or account numbers.
  • The website code is public on GitHub Pages so anyone can read it. Your financial data is not part of that code.
